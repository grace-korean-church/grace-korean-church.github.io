## Who We Are

Grace Korean Church (은혜한인교회) is a Korean-speaking congregation in Moline,
Illinois, serving the Quad Cities area. The church was established on
**August 15, 1982** and is affiliated with the **Assemblies of God**.

We gather to worship, to share life with one another, and to serve our neighbors.
Long-time residents and newcomers alike are welcome.

## Language

Sunday worship is conducted **in Korean, with English interpretation**. There is
also a separate **English Bible study on Wednesday mornings at 10:00 AM**, which
is a good place to start if you do not speak Korean.
